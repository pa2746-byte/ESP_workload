Main findings: 
7
Pipeline is working end-to-end:
CUDA run → Nsight capture → stats/trace parse → flow JSON creation.
Transfer pattern differences are reflected structurally:
per_iter graphs are longer/repetitive chains.
once graphs are shorter with boundary transfers.
Expected vs measured note:
Synthetic graphs preserve designed byte formulas.
Nsight-derived vol depends on available trace columns in your Nsight version.
